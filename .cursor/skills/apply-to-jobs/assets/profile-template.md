# Applicant profile

Filled in during the interview and reused on every later run. Editing this file directly is expected — pay expectations, certifications, and target roles all change, and updating a line here is faster than sitting through the interview again.

Leave a field blank rather than guessing. A blank field makes Claude ask or skip; a guessed one makes it misrepresent.
