FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior coding experience for Manual Testing?
Absolutely not! Manual Testing is perfect for beginners. No coding knowledge required. We start from scratch and cover everything from software basics to professional test case writing. Many successful alumni came from non-IT backgrounds like commerce, arts, and science.
Is ISTQB certification included in the course fee?
ISTQB exam prep materials, mock tests, and coaching are included in Manual and Automation Testing programs. The ISTQB exam fee (approximately ₹7,500) is paid separately to ISTQB. We guarantee you'll be 100% exam-ready.
What does "100% Placement Assistance" mean exactly?
Our placement assistance includes: resume building and optimization, LinkedIn profile makeover, mock technical and HR interviews, portfolio review, and direct referrals to our 450+ hiring partners. Our 2024 batch had a 96% placement rate within 90 days of course completion.
Can I get an EMI for the course fee?
Yes! We offer No Cost EMI (0% interest) through HDFC, ICICI, Axis, and Razorpay. Starting from ₹3,500/month for Manual Testing. You can also pay in 2–3 instalments directly to us with no extra charge. Contact our counselor for the exact EMI plan.
Are the classes recorded? What if I miss a session?
Yes, every live session is recorded and uploaded to our student portal within 4 hours. You get lifetime access to all recordings, notes, and materials. You can also attend any missed topic in the next batch free of cost.
Do I get a certificate after completing the course?
Yes! You receive a Tech1World Course Completion Certificate recognized by our 450+ hiring partners. For the internship program, you also receive an LOR and internship certificate. All certificates are digitally verifiable with QR codes.
Is the internship open to students from other colleges/institutions?
The internship program is primarily for our course graduates, but we occasionally accept external candidates for specific projects through a screening test. Priority is always given to our students. Contact us to discuss your situation.
